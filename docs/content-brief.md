### Overview

**IT services & consulting for enterprise content**  
Established October 2006, J.S. One Sdn Bhd is a Malaysia-based IT services and consulting company specializing in OpenText ECM and SAP solutions.

As an authorized OpenText reseller and certified implementation partner, we deliver consultancy, implementation, AMS, and system development.

Image callout: Make A Better Tomorrow

### Mission

We are constantly focusing on innovating and believe that we need to own and control the primary technologies behind the solution that we make, and participate only in markets where we can make a significant contribution.

### Vision

Our vision is to be Asia’s most customer centric company; to build a solution where customer value and embrace.

### Core value

Acknowledgement and appreciation of each customer’s individual uniqueness needs and culture.
